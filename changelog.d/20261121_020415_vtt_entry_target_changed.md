### Fixed

- A same-size Itanium VTT (`_ZTT`) whose entry was retargeted to another vtable address point compared as `NO_CHANGE` with complete assurance, although an old client's virtual call during construction changed its target. On x86-64 ELF, abicheck now reads each exported VTT entry's logical target (the table it points into and the address-point offset, resolved from `R_X86_64_64`, `R_X86_64_RELATIVE` or `DT_RELR`) and reports a changed one as the new BREAKING kind `vtt_entry_target_changed`. A relink that only moves the tables is not reported. An entry whose target cannot be resolved (for example a stripped local construction vtable), or a snapshot written before the fact existed, is declined and named in the analysis assurance notes instead of being read as unchanged. The targets persist as the `abicheck.elf_vtt_entries` extension fact of the `binary` section.

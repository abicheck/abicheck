### Fixed

- **Closed a second quadratic-time regression in `type_reachability.directly_referenced_stdlib_type_spellings`'s record-reachability walk, for records that stay alias-only forever** (Codex review, fresh evidence, performance). The previous fix closed the quadratic case for a record that becomes unconditionally trusted (`_record_direct`); this same shape still occurred for a record that never does — once it had already been walked at least once, a burst of distinct typedef-alias provenance upgrades arriving before its first requeue was even popped appended one worklist entry *per upgrade* regardless, so a record reached via hundreds of distinct aliases (all discovered while walking a *different*, already-popped record's own fields) queued that many duplicate entries, each pop rescanning under every accumulated alias — quadratic (a reported repro with 800 aliases took roughly 4.4 seconds, doubled since contract evaluation scans both comparison sides). `_StdlibReferenceScan` now tracks whether a record already has a pending worklist entry and skips appending a duplicate one; a pop always rescans with every provenance accumulated up to that point regardless of how many upgrades arrived while it was pending, so one pending entry per record is always enough.

### Fixed

- **`anon_fields` detector registration order restored after the
  `diff_symbols_anon_fields.py` split** — the earlier extraction moved the
  `@registry.detector("anon_fields")`-decorated function itself into the
  new leaf module, which silently changed its registration order (and
  therefore its position in every JSON/text report) relative to
  `diff_symbols.py`'s other detectors, since registration happens when a
  decorated function is defined, not when its body is later called
  (Codex review on PR #980). `_diff_anon_fields` now stays defined and
  decorated in `diff_symbols.py` at its original source position, calling
  the moved loop-body helpers — the identical split shape
  `diff_param_qualifiers.py` already established for `param_restrict`/
  `param_va_list`, and the exact invariant
  `tests/test_detector_discovery.py::test_param_qualifier_detectors_keep_their_registration_position`
  already pins for that case.

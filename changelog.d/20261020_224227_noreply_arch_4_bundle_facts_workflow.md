### Changed

- **Stored-bundle-facts `compare` rules and orchestration moved behind the workflow interface.** The option rejections that decide whether a stored OLD_INPUT can honor a flag now live in `workflows/bundle_facts_stored_request.py` as a typed `BundleFactsCompareRequest` and a Click-free `validate_bundle_facts_request()`, and the comparison runs through `execute_bundle_facts_compare()` in `workflows/bundle_facts_stored_compare.py`. The CLI keeps flag parsing, rendering, file writes and the exit mapping. Exit codes, messages and output are unchanged; the rules are now testable without a Click context. `compare_bundle_facts_rejections.py` is removed (its rules are in the workflow module; `STORED_BUNDLE_FACTS_FORMATS` and `resolve_max_json_object_nodes_cfg` moved with them).

### Fixed

- **Mach-O vtable and thunk layout checks** — the binary-only layout detectors
  (`VTABLE_SLOT_COUNT_CHANGED`, `VTABLE_THUNK_OFFSET_CHANGED`,
  `VTABLE_THUNK_SET_CHANGED`, `RTTI_INHERITANCE_CHANGED`) now read macOS dylibs
  as well as ELF. A Mach-O `nlist` has no size, so each `_ZTV`/`_ZTI`/`_ZTT`
  size is derived from the next defined symbol of its section, recorded as
  `MachoExport.size`. A pair with one ELF side and one Mach-O side is not
  compared by these detectors.

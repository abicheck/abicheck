### Changed

- **The native dump dispatches through one binary-format seam** — `run_dump` now hands each format's primary extraction to an adapter registered in `abicheck.service_dump_native.FORMAT_ADAPTERS` (protocol and request type: `BinaryFormatAdapter`, `NativeExtractRequest` in `abicheck.workflows.dump.formats`) instead of calling private `_dump_elf`/`_dump_pe`/`_dump_macho` functions by name. Dump output is unchanged. The PE and Mach-O extractors moved to `abicheck.workflows.dump.pe` (`extract_pe`, `extract_pdb_debug`) and `abicheck.workflows.dump.macho` (`extract_macho`); `abicheck/service_dump_native_pe.py` is deleted. The ELF extractor is `abicheck.service_dump_native.extract_elf`. `abicheck.service` no longer re-exports the undocumented `_dump_elf`, `_dump_pe`, `_dump_macho` and `_run_dump_uncached`; to substitute an extractor, replace its `FORMAT_ADAPTERS` entry instead of patching those names.

### Added

- **`ChangeKindRegistry` now validates policy-override references and
  default-verdict consistency at import time** — ADR-061 D9's "valid
  references" and "non-contradictory defaults" catalog-validation
  properties. A `ChangeKindMeta.policy_overrides` entry naming an unknown
  policy, targeting `strict_abi` (whose verdict is `default_verdict`
  itself), or restating `default_verdict` verbatim now raises `ValueError`,
  as does `is_addition=True` combined with a `default_verdict` other than
  `Verdict.COMPATIBLE`. `VALID_BASE_POLICIES` moved from `checker_policy.py`
  to the leaf `change_registry_types.py` (re-exported unchanged from
  `checker_policy`) so the new checks avoid an import cycle.

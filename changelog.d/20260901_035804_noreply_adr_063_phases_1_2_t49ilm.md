### Changed

- **`Change.entity_id` now populated across the type-diff detectors** —
  every struct/union/enum/typedef-family finding from `diff_types.py` and
  `diff_types_field_facts.py` (type/field/base/vtable/enum-member changes)
  now carries the matched `RecordType`/`EnumType`/`Function`'s `entity_id`
  where one is available, continuing ADR-063 Phase 2's exhaustive
  population beyond the function-diff path. Field-level findings attribute
  to their containing record's identity (fields have no `EntityId` of
  their own yet). `entity_id` remains `compare=False` and additive-only
  (folded into `resolve_change_identity` as a non-primary alias), so no
  existing suppression rule, canonical finding ID, or report shape
  changes. Typedef/constant-family findings are unaffected — no
  `EntityId` producer exists for them yet.

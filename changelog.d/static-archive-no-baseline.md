### Added

- **`compare --no-baseline libfoo.a -H include/` audits a static archive** — an ELF `ar` archive is now accepted as the single-build audit operand (ADR-086). Its own symbol index (global and weak definitions of its members, C++ names mangled) is the export table, so `public_not_exported` and `exported_not_public` run on archive-only products such as lp64/ilp64 variants. An archive without an index fails with exit 64 and a `ranlib` hint instead of reading as "defines nothing"; thin, Mach-O, COFF and bitcode-member archives are refused with a reason; `unversioned_exported_symbol` reports `not_collected`. `dump` and two-sided `compare` still reject archives. The `ar` index parser moved from `abicheck.buildsource.archive_graph` to `abicheck.extract.ar_archive` / `abicheck.extract.ar_archive_format`.

### Fixed

- **Enum end-of-list markers are recognised in every spelling.** `is_sentinel_enum_member` now tokenizes names on `_`/`::` and camelCase boundaries and recognises `*_last`, `*_max`, `*_count`, `*_end`, `*_num`, `*_sentinel`, two-token tails such as `*_last_symbol`/`LastSymbol`/`*_MAX_VALUE`, and a leading `NUM_*`/`kNum*` marker. oneDNN's `dnnl_graph_op_last_symbol` and C++ `kind::LastSymbol` shifting after an inserted member are now reported as `enum_last_member_value_changed`, not `enum_member_value_changed`.
- **`serialization_tag_changed` no longer fires on layout enums or end markers.** A bare `_tag` suffix on an enum *type* (e.g. oneDNN's `format_tag`) is no longer treated as a serialization-id registry; a type now needs `*_serialization_tag`, `SerializationTag`, or `*_tag_id`, classified identically across C typedef (`*_t`), C++ and CamelCase spellings. Sentinel members are never treated as tag ids, removing the duplicate verdict on `format_tag_last`.

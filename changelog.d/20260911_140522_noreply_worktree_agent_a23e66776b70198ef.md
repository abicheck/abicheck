### Fixed

- **Assurance-overlay `compile.nostdinc` merge now matches the real
  `merge_compile_config` OR semantics.** `_merge_compile_block` (the
  single-sided `mode: compare` promotion helper added for `analysis.
  assurance: complete`) previously classified `compile.nostdinc` alongside
  `compile.frontend_context` as "later-folded document wins outright" —
  correct for `frontend_context`, but not for `nostdinc`: the real two-call
  pipeline (`cli_compare_helpers.py`'s `nostdinc_explicit=
  _nostdinc_explicit or compile_context.nostdinc` feeding `cli_options.
  merge_compile_config`) preserves an already-`true` checkout value across
  the sources-root fold, equivalent to `checkout or sources`. A stored
  checkout `compile.nostdinc: true` paired with a sources-root `compile.
  nostdinc: false` was silently collapsing to `false` in the synthesized
  overlay, changing the header include environment (and potentially the
  observed API/findings) purely because `analysis.assurance: complete`
  triggered this promotion path.

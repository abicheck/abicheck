### Fixed

- **The v1-to-v2 `EntityId` wire-migration adapter no longer collides two
  distinct legacy identities that differ only in an empty `::`-separated
  qualified-name component.** `"A::B"` and `"A::::B"` are two structurally
  different, equally legal values the pre-existing wire shape places no
  grammar restriction against; both are now preserved as distinct scopes
  instead of the empty component being silently dropped.

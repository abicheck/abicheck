### Added

- `.abicheck.yml` `scope:` accepts `dependencies` (named dependency header
  roots), `private_headers` and `private_namespaces`. `abicheck dump
  --dry-run` shows the ownership rules and each `-H` header's owner and
  contract, and warns when a `-H` header is not public target API. The keys
  are previewed only: no dump keeps, drops or reclassifies a declaration
  because of them yet.

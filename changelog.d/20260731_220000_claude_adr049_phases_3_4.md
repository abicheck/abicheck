### Added

- **ADR-049 Phase 3's provider-evidence ledger.** `compare(...,
  contract_evaluation=True)` now records *which evidence* each shadow
  contract-relevance decision rests on. A new leaf module,
  `abicheck/contract_evidence_collect.py`, emits one `EvidenceSearchRecord`
  per (provider, side) — `public_header`, `export_table`, and the
  `post_manifest`/`forced_public_symbols` overlays when configured — each
  with its own status, completeness, identity coverage, requested-vs-searched
  scope, and content digest. Every stamped finding carries
  `contract_evidence_refs` (serialized in the JSON report alongside the
  existing `contract_relevance`/`contract_reason_code`/`contract_assurance`
  fields), naming the records its decision used; a non-entity finding
  correctly cites none. Provider failures stay scoped: an unavailable header
  surface no longer says anything about a completed export-table search.
- **ADR-049 Phase 4's persisted contract context, with replay and
  re-evaluation.** A `--contract-evaluation` JSON report now carries a
  `contract_context` block: the policy-independent `contract_evidence`
  (including a real raw type graph, not a placeholder), the resolved
  `evaluation_context` with field-level provenance, and the
  `decision_receipt`. `abicheck/contract_context_io.py` round-trips the group
  byte- and order-independently, and `abicheck/contract_replay.py` adds the
  two procedures ADR-049 D6 names: `replay_original_decisions()` reproduces a
  recorded decision from the receipt alone (current provider defaults cannot
  alter it), and `reevaluate_from_evidence()` re-decides findings from the
  same persisted observations under a *different* contract mode, with no
  re-collection and no live re-probe. A version counter newer than this build
  understands fails closed; a mixed-version context (older evidence, current
  policy) is the ordinary re-evaluation case and is accepted.
- **`scripts/measure_contract_shadow.py`** — ADR-049 Phase 3's measurement and
  gate. Runs the labelled FP-rate corpus through the shadow evaluator in all
  three contract domains and reports the delta matrix, unresolved rate by
  provider/domain/platform, proven public-break losses, and proven
  false-positive reductions; gates on zero losses, zero unevidenced deltas,
  and zero findings without a recorded decision. Mirrored into the unit lane
  by `tests/test_contract_shadow_measurement.py`.

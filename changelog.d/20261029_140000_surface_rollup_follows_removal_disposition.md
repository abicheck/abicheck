### Fixed

- **A public-surface roll-up follows the removals it summarises** — removing a public function, variable or type also produced a `public_surface_shrank` roll-up (symbol `<surface>`) that no rule could name, so a suppression of the removal hid the removal but left the roll-up in the report: the run read as a COMPATIBLE change instead of `NO_CHANGE`, and the roll-up was counted. The roll-up is now hidden, with the suppression rule recorded, when every removal it covers is suppressed, and it is acknowledged when every covered removal is acknowledged. It stays visible, unchanged, while any covered removal does, and the membership (ELF-only variants included) is owned by `ROLLUP_MEMBER_KINDS` next to the variant relation. Headerless and `-H` runs are both affected.

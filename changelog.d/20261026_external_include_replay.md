### Fixed

- **MSVC/clang-cl `/external:I` include operands are carried into the replayed argv.** The L4 source-replay scanner dropped `/external:I <dir>`, `/external:I<dir>` and the `-external:I` spellings, so a system-include directory the real compile searched was missing from the replay, which could make the parse fail or resolve different headers. Those operands are now carried verbatim, as `/I` already is, because the replay driver (clang in `--driver-mode=cl`) accepts `/external:I` itself. Other `/external:` options are unchanged. Affected source-tier replays re-record under a new `SOURCE_GENERATION`.

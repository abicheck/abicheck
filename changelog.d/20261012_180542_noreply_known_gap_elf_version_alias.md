### Fixed

- **Binding, visibility, size and symbol-state changes on symbol-versioned aliases are no longer hidden** — a bare symbol exported under several versions (`foo@VER1` and `foo@@VER2`) was compared only through its last `.dynsym` entry, so a `GLOBAL`→`WEAK` or visibility change on the other alias went unreported, and so did a TLS size, COMMON, UNIQUE, protected-visibility, RTTI or leaked-dependency change on it. Aliases now pair old and new by version tag. A declaration whose aliases disagree on binding or visibility records neither value. Stored snapshots are cleared the same way on load, and unpairable aliases are listed as declined comparisons. Single-version libraries are unchanged.

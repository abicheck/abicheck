### Fixed

- **A typed `CompareRequest` combining a real `policy_file_path` with `pack_policy_overrides`/`pack_internal_namespaces` could lose the real file's own identity from its receipt.** The previous fix cleared the merged `PolicyFile`'s `source_path`/`source_sha256` whenever a pack contributed anything, to stop misattributing the pack's own override to the file — but that also discarded the file's own, independently-real provenance, even when the file had genuinely contributed entries of its own. The receipt now names both contributors explicitly: `policy.overrides` (additive, like a real `--pack` manifest) keeps the file's own `path`/`sha256` and records the forwarded pack's contribution as an additional `selected_by` hop; `surface.internal_namespaces` (a full replace, not a merge) is attributed entirely to the forwarded pack when one sets it, since crediting the file there would be false whenever a pack actually overwrote it.

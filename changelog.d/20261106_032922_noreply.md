### Performance

- **Header-graph projection runs with cyclic GC paused** -- the projection's full-tree AST walks (type, call, semantic-fact and body passes) no longer rescan the live AST heap on every generation-2 collection. Measured on a 10x synthetic clang AST: first projection 8.4 s -> 5.5 s; the projection output is unchanged (regression test `tests/test_header_graph_projection_gc.py`).

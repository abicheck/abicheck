### Added

- **`Change.entity_id` (ADR-063 Phase 2) now populated across the
  layout/vtable-layout/variable detectors and the remaining `diff_symbols.py`
  gaps** — `diff_layout.py`'s six layout-descriptor findings,
  `diff_vtable_layout.py`'s two vtable-group findings,
  `diff_symbols_variables.py`'s alignment/access-level findings, and
  nine previously-unwired `diff_symbols.py` sites (`VAR_TYPE_CHANGED`,
  the const-transition pair, `VAR_REMOVED`/`VAR_ADDED`,
  `FIELD_ACCESS_CHANGED`, both `ANON_FIELD_CHANGED` branches, and
  `VAR_DEPRECATED_ADDED`/`REMOVED`) all now carry the matched `RecordType`/
  `Variable`'s resolved identity, following the existing old-preferred/
  new-fallback convention. The three `CONSTANT_*` findings stay unwired —
  `AbiSnapshot.constants` is a plain `dict[str, str]` with no parsed
  declaration behind it to carry an `EntityId`. Still `compare=False`, so
  no equality-based behavior changes.

### Added

- **Compiled target ISA as a root-cause finding** — a new `TARGET_ISA_CHANGED` (RISK) reports when the two builds were compiled for different instruction-set targets, read from the `-march=`/`-m<feature>` flags in `DW_AT_producer` and the `GNU_PROPERTY_X86_ISA_1_NEEDED` note (e.g. `-mavx2` vs `-mavx512f`, or an x86-64-v2 → v3 bump). Layout and symbol findings that such a change plausibly causes are annotated with a clause naming it; none are hidden or re-classified. Nothing is reported when either side's target is unknown (clang, stripped producer).

### Changed

- **Internal only: probe-matrix JSON I/O moved out of `probe_harness.py`** —
  `ProbeResult`/`MatrixSnapshot`'s snapshot (de)serialization (previously
  `ProbeResult.to_dict()`, `MatrixSnapshot.to_json()`/`.from_dict()`, and the
  module-level `write_matrix_snapshot`/`load_matrix_snapshot`) now lives in
  `abicheck.workflows.findings` instead of `abicheck.probe_harness`
  (ADR-061 gap E: `probe_harness.py` is `compare`-classified and may not own
  a `storage` operation). `probe_harness.py` is an internal module, not
  documented Python API, so no compatibility shim was kept at the old path;
  behavior and JSON shape are unchanged.

### Changed

- **Three more change kinds declare where they are observable** (ADR-093 WS-1 batch 5) — `soname_bump_recommended`, `soname_bump_unnecessary` and `visibility_leak` now carry an `observable_at` declaration of the binary evidence (`{B}`, ELF). Each declaration is proven by a real GNU ELF build run through `abicheck dump` and `abicheck compare`, with a negative control. Nothing about what the tool reports changes; the three kinds leave the UNCLASSIFIED baseline.

### Fixed

- **Closed a quadratic-time regression in `type_reachability.directly_referenced_stdlib_type_spellings`'s record-reachability walk** (Codex review, fresh evidence, performance). Once a record was already unconditionally trusted (`_record_direct`), a further typedef alias that also reached it still grew `_record_typedef_origins` and requeued the record for a rescan — even though the unconditional direct scan already finds every match at the strongest trust tier, so a new alias could never add anything the direct scan wouldn't already find. A public record with hundreds of alias-typed fields all targeting an already-directly-reached record queued the same identity once per distinct alias, and each pop rescanned every field text under every accumulated alias — quadratic in alias count (a reported repro with 1,200 aliases took roughly 12 seconds, doubled since contract evaluation scans both comparison sides). `_StdlibReferenceScan.reach_record` now stops accumulating and requeuing on a typedef-alias reach once a record is already direct, and `_walk_reached_records` skips the now-redundant per-alias rescan loop entirely for an already-direct record, relying solely on the single unconditional scan.

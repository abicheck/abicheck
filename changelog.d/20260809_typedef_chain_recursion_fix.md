### Fixed

- **Fixed a `RecursionError` `type_reachability._StdlibReferenceScan` could raise on a long chain of typedef aliases** (Codex review, fresh evidence) — a public signature naming the outermost alias of a chain several hundred aliases deep (`A0 -> A1 -> A2 -> ... -> std::string`) drove `scan`'s own recursive alias-following past Python's default recursion limit, and a snapshot exposing such a chain deepest-first (each alias named by its own, separate public declaration) hit the identical failure mode in `_propagate_typedef_provenance`'s own recursive call. Both are now driven by an explicit worklist instead of the call stack, mirroring `_finditer_allow_nested`'s own stack-based rewrite for the same class of unbounded-nesting risk — confirmed empirically fixed against a several-thousand-alias chain in both shapes. `contract_pipeline.build_contract_stage` invokes this scan for every `--contract-evaluation` comparison, so this previously aborted the whole comparison rather than producing a verdict.

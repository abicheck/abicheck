### Fixed

- **A stack canary that an optimized rebuild no longer needs is not reported as a hardening loss when debug info proves the flag unchanged** — an ELF dump now records every compilation unit's stack-protector setting from its `DW_AT_producer` (gcc records it by default, clang with `-grecord-command-line`). A `stack_canary_removed` finding is demoted to `COMPATIBLE` with rule `stack-protector-unchanged-in-every-unit` when every unit on both sides records the protector still on and no weaker. It keeps its RISK when the recorded flag was weakened, and also when no unit records it (binary only), where `review_evidence.note` says the evidence is weaker. Extractor generation 46; re-dump stored baselines to pick it up.
- **An exported object's alignment derived from its address no longer reads as a reduction by itself** — the address only bounds the declared alignment, so `exported_object_alignment_reduced` is demoted to `COMPATIBLE` (rules `address-alignment-without-declared-requirement`, `address-alignment-meets-declared-requirement`) unless the old side declares an alignment the new placement fails. The finding stays in the report. A dropped `_Alignas(16)` still reports under DWARF, whose `DW_AT_alignment` on a variable is now extracted, and under clang headers.

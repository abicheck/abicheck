### Fixed

- **The match cache's per-pattern retention estimate is now calibrated against real patterns rather than reasoned from first principles.** `_PATTERN_BYTES_PER_CHAR` was 6, from "the `str` plus roughly twice the text again". Measured against the six vocabularies a real oneDAL comparison compiles — patterns spanning 8,657 to 3,336,273 characters — that undercounts by a strikingly stable **1.46–1.51×** (19.09 MB estimated vs 27.94 MB measured on the largest). It is now 9, tracking the measured ~8.8 bytes/char, and remains an explicit *lower* bound since `sys.getsizeof` on a compiled pattern does not reach its compiled program's internal allocations. Erring low is the wrong direction for a budget whose job is to bound growth: it lets the cache retain more than it is allowed to while reporting that it did not.
